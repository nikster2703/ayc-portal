☠️ Danger Zone

These actions are catastrophic and irreversible. They should only ever be used to completely reset the portal between seasons or before a fresh import. A backup is strongly recommended first.

Clear All Members
Permanently deletes all members, their contacts, alert flags, attendance records, and DofE participant data. Portal users, documents and settings are not affected.
☠️ Clear All Members?

This will permanently delete every member record in the database, along with all associated contacts, alert flags, attendance history, and DofE data.

This action cannot be undone. Type DELETE ALL MEMBERS to confirm:

⚠️ Restore Database?

This will replace the live database with the uploaded backup. The current database will be saved automatically to data/backups/ before the swap.

All users will be logged out automatically after the restore.
