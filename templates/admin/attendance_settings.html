📱 Register & QR Sign-In

QR Quick Sign-In / Sign-Out

When enabled, a QR code appears on the reception display. Members scan it with their phone to sign in (or out) without queuing at the iPad. The QR code is automatically invalidated when the register is completed or regenerated by a leader.

⚠️ Safeguarding note: Only enable QR sign-out if your organisation does not require a guardian to be physically present at sign-out. For youth clubs where a parent or carer must collect the child, leave this off — sign-out should be confirmed by a leader at the iPad.
